How it works

One shared list for the whole family. Everyone can see it, add to it and edit it. Changes show up for everyone right away.

Add something you want

  1. Tap + Add item.
  2. Type your name and the item name.
  3. Fill in what you know: category, price in pesos, link, priority, a photo and notes such as size or color.
  4. Tap Save.

Edit or remove an item

Tap Edit on the item. Change anything and tap Save. To remove it, tap Delete and confirm. Please only delete your own items.

Planning to buy something for someone? Reserve it

  1. Find the item and change its Status to Reserved.
  2. Type your name when asked. It shows on the item so nobody buys the same thing.
  3. After you buy it, change Status to Purchased. After it has been given, change it to Received.

Reserved items turn soft gold, Purchased soft green and Received soft blue-gray. If you see one of those, pick a different gift.

Product link and photo

Paste the link from Shopee, Lazada, Amazon or any shop into the Link box. To add a photo, tap Choose photo and pick a picture from your phone or computer.

Find things fast

Use the search box and the person, category and status menus above the list.

New family member

Just type a new name when adding an item. They appear in the menus and in the Overview automatically.
